How it works

Play free resources from your hand. Or end your turn without playing a card: a random Pillar or Tower from your remaining deck enters play. Pillars skip that production, but Towers produce normally during the same turn pass. Neither generates quanta simply from entering.

Production order: Pillars and Towers first, Pendulums last.

Pendulum rule: Each one produces its assigned element, then the majority Pillar/Tower element, then repeats. Ties are independently random when the Pendulum produces. If no elemental Pillar or Tower exists, it defaults to its assigned element for that production.

    Card Workshop

    Load any approved Air or Aether card for fast manual testing. Debug effects are clearly separate from legitimate deck actions.

    The workshop respects the eight-card hand limit. Debug-created cards bypass the ordinary 60-card deck and copy limit by design.

    Opponent Test Controls

    Practice against grounded physical attackers or an Airborne Psion. Freeze targets to test Shockwave, or destroy one of your permanents to test Salvaging.

    No opponent AI is implemented yet. The dummy's creatures attack your player on each turn pass.
